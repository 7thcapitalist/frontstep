## Published Frontstep — baseline, not the upper ambition

Source: https://frontstep-phi.vercel.app/

The opening screen combines very large, tightly composed black type, a quiet grid, warm neutral material, and a blue glass object behind the headline. The effect is a scene with depth, not a small illustration inside an otherwise empty split hero. The headline remains the reading anchor and the action is obvious.

The examples deliberately change visual language by niche:

- **PISADA:** the product enters and rotates as a real 3D model; color chips change its material. Huge outlined background type gives scale, while the actual commercial grid below uses real photographs. This is appropriate only with a valid product/model asset. Do not fake a business product with an arbitrary shoe.
- **BRASA:** real burger photography expands from a framed composition into a larger scene with scroll. The menu has a photo interaction tied to individual items. Keep product labels truthful when stock is only illustrative.
- **Ateliê Camélia:** a clearly illustrative floral scene grows with scroll, followed by real bouquet photographs. The generated/abstract scene expresses a concept; it is not documentary evidence of products.
- **Café Grão:** particles form the brand from steam. This is a niche-specific physical metaphor. Do not reuse steam for accounting, healthcare, industry or every other business.

Transferable lesson: **one memorable scene plus a useful interactive feature**, supported by excellent type, varied rhythm and real visual material. Do not put a glass blob, particle field or shoe on every site. Do not copy the portfolio's long demonstration scroll onto a short local-business page. Keep native scroll and a compact mobile variant.

## Direction by family

| Family | Scene worth designing | Functional continuation |
|---|---|---|
| Food | Large real-photo window, cropped type, a menu/photo transition | Order builder with immediate quantity/total feedback |
| Accounting/legal | Overlapping document/paper layers resolving into an organized summary | Compact triage, selections visible in a live summary |
| Health | Calm spatial composition or gently connecting care path; truthful still imagery | Service/visit preparation, clear next step without diagnosing |
| Beauty | Editorial portrait/detail frame, elegant aperture or image panel transition | Service selection with a composed appointment summary |
| Fitness | Strong kinetic typography or schedule track; energetic but readable | Filtered timetable with immediate useful changes |
| Shop | Product/material editorial spread; change view/detail on selection | Basket or quote list, visible selected items and correction |
| Hospitality | A real landscape window that opens with scroll, layered travel typography | Stay/event inquiry with clear dates and summary |
| Industry | Technical line/part assembly, process sequence connected to specifications | Quote configurator showing actual selected inputs |
| Local service | An exploded tool/repair diagram or service-region transition | Problem triage and useful visit summary |

Choose a scene that the actual assets can support. A drawing can be conceptually clear without pretending to be a product rendering. The user explicitly rejected obvious AI photorealism; use licensed real photos or an intentionally nonphotographic visual solution.
