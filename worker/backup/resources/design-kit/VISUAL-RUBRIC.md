# Independent visual standard — October 16, 2026

Evaluate a **synthetic demonstration** against its brief and available assets. Commercial readiness is a separate question. A fictitious business can have excellent design; never award or deduct points merely for being a disclosed demo. Never require invented social proof, people or credentials to raise a score. Real stock photography is permitted as clearly illustrative material; conspicuous AI photorealism is not.

Use one decimal place, not integer-only ratings. The worker calculates the weighted total from these seven criteria, requires a total **strictly above 9.0**, and requires every criterion to be at least 8.5. A numerical result does not override a major defect. The reviewer must explain the actual visual evidence. This is an internal design assessment, not a universal or customer rating.

| Criterion | Weight | What earns a strong result |
|---|---:|---|
| artDirection | 20% | Immediately recognizable idea rooted in this business. Distinct silhouette, material, framing or visual narrative; not interchangeable with unrelated niches. |
| typography | 15% | Deliberate display/body contrast, excellent proportions, useful line breaks, polished labels and readable text at every size. |
| composition | 15% | Strong first viewport, intentional negative space, image/type relationship, at least three section rhythms, no dead page length. |
| imagery | 10% | Beautiful and truthful use of available material, intentional crops and harmonized color. A strong typographic or diagrammatic solution may earn full marks where photos are unsuitable. |
| motion | 15% | A meaningful signature scene demonstrably changes across browser frames, with coherent timing and interaction feedback. Native scroll, immediate controls, complete reduced-motion and no-JS states. A hover alone is insufficient. |
| interaction | 15% | The niche feature is useful, polished in empty/populated/corrected states, feedback is visible, selections and next action are close, keyboard works. |
| responsive | 10% | Desktop and mobile are deliberately composed. No clipped type, unreadable credits, awkward stacking, accidental whitespace, occluded controls or horizontal overflow. |

## Calibration

- 6–7: usable but visibly generic or unresolved.
- 8–8.4: solid professional basics; clear weaknesses in distinctiveness, rhythm, image handling or flow.
- 8.5–9.0: strong work, but one or more noticeable craft decisions still limit the experience.
- 9.1–9.5: excellent, distinctive and coherent; remaining observations are small polish, with no important defect.
- 9.6–10: exceptional execution throughout, including transitions and interactive states. Reserve for evidence that warrants it.

Do not give a high score merely because QA passed, GSAP exists, many skills were read, a previous attempt scored well, or the user wants a high score. Do not hold a synthetic local-business preview to invented requirements absent from its brief. Reference images show composition, not facts to reuse.

For every failed review, list the highest-impact concrete changes first. Name the section, current visible problem, target composition and intended benefit. Avoid vague 'make it premium' advice and do not alternate incompatible art directions between attempts without explaining the evidence. No changing scores just to end a repair loop.
