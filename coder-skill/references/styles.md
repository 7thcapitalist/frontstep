# Style directions

Starting points, not templates. Pick the one that fits the niche and the brief's `vibe`, then make it specific to the business (its colors, its street, its product). If none fits, define a new direction in plan.md. STATUS: draft, waiting on Joao's picks; he has asked for more research-driven, big-tech-grade options.
